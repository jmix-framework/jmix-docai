# GENERATOR SUPPLY AGREEMENT No. 19
## Standby diesel generator with installation

This Generator Supply Agreement is made on {signed:long} between Standby Power Systems LLC, 31 Stockton Road, Springfield, IL 62711 (the “Supplier”), and Meadowbrook LLC, 12 Garden Street, Suite 312, Springfield, IL 62701 (the “Buyer”).

1. The Supplier sells and installs, and the Buyer buys, a 60 kW (75 kVA) standby diesel generator in a sound-attenuated enclosure, with an automatic transfer switch that starts it when utility power fails. The generator will power the warehouse at 36 Commerce Avenue, Building 2, Springfield, IL 62702.
2. Price: $20,800.00. The Buyer pays a 50% advance of $10,400.00 within 3 business days after signing this Agreement and the balance after signing the certificate of completion.
3. The Supplier delivers the generator, installs it on a concrete pad and connects it within 30 business days of the advance payment.
4. Warranty: 12 months or 1,000 engine hours, whichever comes first.
