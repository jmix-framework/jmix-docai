# COURIER SERVICES AGREEMENT No. 25

This Courier Services Agreement is made on {signed:long} between Capital City Couriers LLC, 32 Nichols Street, Springfield, IL 62703 (the “Service Provider”), and Meadowbrook LLC, 12 Garden Street, Suite 312, Springfield, IL 62701 (the “Customer”).

1. At the Customer’s request, the Service Provider delivers documents and small packages within the Springfield city limits. Requests are taken by email on business days from 9:00 a.m. to 5:00 p.m.
2. Delivery time: within 4 hours of pickup; rush delivery within 2 hours.
3. A standard delivery costs $4.30 and a rush delivery $8.80. The Customer pays within 5 business days after receiving the monthly statement of deliveries.
4. The Service Provider hands documents only to the addressee or an authorized representative, who signs for them, and returns the signed proof of delivery to the Customer.
