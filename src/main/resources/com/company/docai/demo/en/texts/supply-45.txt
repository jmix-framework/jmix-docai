# SUPPLY AGREEMENT No. 45
## Office paper and office supplies

This Supply Agreement is made on {signed:long} between Northwind Paper Inc., 157 Northfield Drive, Unit 6, Springfield, IL 62702 (the “Supplier”), and Meadowbrook LLC, 12 Garden Street, Suite 312, Springfield, IL 62701 (the “Buyer”).

1. During {year:signed}, the Supplier will sell the Buyer office paper and office supplies (the “Goods”) as the Buyer orders them.
2. Each order and the Supplier’s invoice state the items, quantities and prices of the order. Prices are those of the Supplier’s price list on the order date.
3. The estimated value of this Agreement is $10,700.00. If deliveries reach this amount before the Agreement expires, the parties sign an amendment to increase it.
4. The Buyer places orders by email, and the Supplier confirms them within 1 business day. The Supplier delivers the Goods in its own trucks to the Buyer’s office at 12 Garden Street, Suite 312, Springfield, IL 62701, within 5 business days of the order. Delivery is included in the price.
5. The Buyer pays each invoice within 20 days after delivery.
6. By the 10th of each month, the Supplier sends the Buyer a statement for the previous month.
7. Title to the Goods passes to the Buyer when the Buyer signs the delivery receipt.
8. The Buyer checks the quantity and the items on receipt and reports latent defects within 10 days after delivery; the Supplier replaces defective Goods at its own expense within 5 business days.
9. Copy paper comes in the manufacturer’s packaging: letter size, 20 lb, 92 brightness or higher, 500 sheets per ream.
10. If a delivery is late, the Supplier pays liquidated damages of 0.1% of the value of the undelivered Goods per day; if a payment is late, the Buyer pays a late charge of 0.1% of the overdue amount per day.
11. This Agreement takes effect on the date of signing and remains in effect until {validUntil:long}.
12. This Agreement is governed by Illinois law. Any dispute will be resolved in the state courts of Sangamon County, Illinois.
