# COMMERCIAL LEASE AGREEMENT No. 112

This Commercial Lease Agreement (the “Lease”) is made on ______________ between Westwind Properties LLC, 2 Warehouse Road, Bloomington, IL 61701 (the “Landlord”), and Meadowbrook LLC, 12 Garden Street, Suite 312, Springfield, IL 62701 (the “Tenant”).

## 1. Premises
1.1 The Landlord leases to the Tenant warehouse space of 4,500 sq ft at 4 Warehouse Road, Bloomington, IL 61701 (the “Premises”).
1.2 The Premises are heated and have a 20-ft ceiling, a dock door and a loading ramp. The Tenant uses the Premises as a warehouse for goods and equipment.
1.3 The Landlord represents that it owns the Premises and has the right to lease them to the Tenant.
1.4 The Landlord delivers the Premises to the Tenant on the first day of the term, and the parties sign a move-in inspection report.
## 2. Term
2.1 The term of this Lease is from {start:long} to {validUntil:long}.
2.2 If the Tenant is not in default, it may renew this Lease for a new term by giving the Landlord written notice at least 30 days before the Lease expires.
## 3. Rent and Payments
3.1 The rent is $4,300.00 per month.
3.2 The total rent for the term (12 months) is $51,600.00.
3.3 Rent is due monthly, in advance, on or before the 5th day of the month, by transfer to the Landlord’s bank account.
3.4 Within 5 business days after signing this Lease, the Tenant pays a security deposit equal to one month’s rent, $4,300.00. The Landlord returns the deposit within 10 business days after the Premises are returned or, if the parties agree, applies it to the rent for the last month.
3.5 Utilities (electricity, heat, water and sewer) are not included in the rent; the Tenant pays for them separately, based on meter readings, as invoiced by the Landlord.
3.6 The rent does not change during the term of this Lease.
## 4. Use and Repairs
4.1 The Landlord gives the Tenant 24/7 access to the Premises and truck access to the warehouse doors, and maintains the building systems in good working order.
4.2 The Tenant uses the Premises only as a warehouse and complies with applicable fire codes. At the end of the term, the Tenant returns the Premises in the condition received, ordinary wear and tear excepted.
4.3 The Tenant makes routine repairs at its own expense and makes alterations or improvements only with the Landlord’s written consent.
4.4 The Tenant may sublease the Premises only with the Landlord’s written consent.
## 5. Late Payment and Force Majeure
5.1 If rent is paid late, the Tenant pays a late charge of 0.5% of the overdue amount per day.
5.2 Neither party is liable for a failure to perform caused by force majeure.
## 6. General
6.1 This Lease may be terminated early by written agreement of the parties or as otherwise permitted by law.
6.2 This Lease is governed by Illinois law. Any dispute will be resolved in the state courts of McLean County, Illinois.
