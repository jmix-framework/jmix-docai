# SECURITY SERVICES AGREEMENT No. 12
## Alarm monitoring and response

This Security Services Agreement is made on {signed:long} between Shield Security LLC, 9 Sterling Avenue, Springfield, IL 62702 (the “Security Company”), and Meadowbrook LLC, 12 Garden Street, Suite 312, Springfield, IL 62701 (the “Customer”).

1. The Security Company provides remote alarm monitoring for two of the Customer’s sites: the warehouse at 36 Commerce Avenue, Building 2, Springfield, IL 62702, and the office at 12 Garden Street, Suite 312, Springfield, IL 62701.
2. When an alarm goes off, the Security Company dispatches a patrol officer to the site within 7 minutes, notifies the police as needed and reports the incident to the Customer’s designated contact.
3. Monitoring and response cost $265.00 per month for the warehouse and $155.00 per month for the office, $420.00 per month in total.
4. This Agreement is in effect from {start:long} to {validUntil:long}. Either party may terminate it on 30 days’ notice to the other party.
