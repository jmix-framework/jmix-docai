# FINANCE LEASE AGREEMENT No. L-17/{year:signed}
## Four-wheel-drive tractor

This Finance Lease Agreement is made on {signed:long} between North Point Leasing LLC, 44 Orchard Street, Springfield, IL 62702 (the “Lessor”), and Meadowbrook LLC, 12 Garden Street, Suite 312, Springfield, IL 62701 (the “Lessee”).

1. The Lessor buys from Westfield Tractor Sales LLC a Corwin 742 four-wheel-drive tractor with a diesel engine (serial No. 742-1905), built for heavy fieldwork, and leases it to the Lessee for 48 months.
2. The cost of the tractor is $164,000.00. The Lessee makes a 20% down payment of $32,800.00 within 5 business days after signing this Agreement.
3. The monthly lease payment is $3,740.00, due by the 10th day of each month.
4. The purchase option price is $1,640.00. Title to the tractor passes to the Lessee once all lease payments and the purchase option price are paid.
5. The Lessee insures the tractor and keeps it maintained throughout the lease term.
