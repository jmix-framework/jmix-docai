# SERVICES AGREEMENT No. 7
## Pest control

This Services Agreement is made on {signed:long} between Prairie Pest Solutions Inc., 31 Park Avenue, Suite 12, Springfield, IL 62704 (the “Service Provider”), and Meadowbrook LLC, 12 Garden Street, Suite 312, Springfield, IL 62701 (the “Customer”).

1. The Service Provider will provide monthly pest control for the Customer’s warehouse and office, including general insect control, rodent control and, when needed, fumigation.
2. The services cover the warehouse at 36 Commerce Avenue, Building 2, Springfield, IL 62702, and the office at 12 Garden Street, Suite 312, Springfield, IL 62701.
3. Treatments take place once a month on a date agreed with the Customer at least 3 business days in advance: in the office after business hours, in the warehouse without stopping its operations.
4. The Service Provider uses its own equipment and only EPA-registered products. Along the perimeter of the warehouse, the Service Provider installs tamper-resistant bait stations, keeps a map of them and services them monthly.
5. The work is done by the Service Provider’s licensed technicians, trained in the safe handling of the products used.
6. The Customer gives the technicians access to all areas to be treated and removes food from the break rooms during treatment.
7. After each treatment, the Service Provider makes an entry in the pest control log kept at the Customer’s site and issues a monthly service report. The log records the date, the areas treated, the products used and the recommendations to the Customer.
8. If the Customer reports pest activity between scheduled treatments, the Service Provider makes an extra visit within 2 business days at no extra charge.
9. The fee is $210.00 per month, or $2,520.00 for {year:signed}.
10. The Customer pays monthly, within 10 business days after receiving the monthly service report and invoice.
11. The Service Provider applies its products safely around people and the Customer’s goods and will indemnify the Customer for any damage its services cause.
12. This Agreement takes effect on the date of signing and remains in effect until {validUntil:long}. Either party may terminate it on at least 30 days’ written notice.
