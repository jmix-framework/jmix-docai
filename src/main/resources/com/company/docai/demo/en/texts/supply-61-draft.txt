# SUPPLY AGREEMENT No. 61/{year:signed}
## Office supplies

This Supply Agreement is made on {signed:long} between Market Street Office Supply LLC, 23 Market Street, Suite 3, Springfield, IL 62704 (the “Supplier”), and Meadowbrook LLC, 12 Garden Street, Suite 312, Springfield, IL 62701 (the “Buyer”).

1. The Supplier will sell the Buyer office supplies (the “Goods”) as the Buyer orders them.
2. The Goods include pens, pencils, markers, notepads, file folders, paper clips, staplers, hole punches, sticky notes, letter trays and desk organizers.
3. The Buyer places orders by email, stating the items, the quantities and the requested delivery date. The Supplier confirms each order and issues an invoice within 1 business day.
4. Prices are those of the Supplier’s price list on the order date. The Supplier may change its price list no more than once a quarter, with at least 30 days’ notice to the Buyer.
5. The estimated value of this Agreement is $2,700.00 per year.
6. The Supplier delivers the Goods to the Buyer’s office at 12 Garden Street, Suite 312, Springfield, IL 62701, within 2 business days after confirming the order, with inside delivery at no charge.
7. The Buyer pays for each order within 10 business days after delivery, upon receipt of the invoice and the signed packing slip.
8. The Buyer checks the quantity and the items on receipt and notes any shortage or damage on the packing slip; the Supplier delivers the missing Goods or replaces the defective ones at its own expense within 3 business days.
9. The Goods are new, in the manufacturer’s original packaging. Staplers, hole punches and desk organizers carry a 12-month warranty from delivery.
10. Title to the Goods passes to the Buyer when the Buyer signs the packing slip.
11. If a delivery is late, the Supplier pays liquidated damages of 0.1% of the value of the undelivered Goods per day; if a payment is late, the Buyer pays a late charge of 0.1% of the overdue amount per day.
12. This Agreement takes effect on the date of signing and remains in effect until {validUntil:long}.
13. This Agreement is governed by Illinois law. Any dispute will be resolved in the state courts of Sangamon County, Illinois.
